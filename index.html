The clinic

How a 3-hour clinic works

Every clinic follows the same proven structure: prepare properly, swim with purpose, then see and understand the evidence.

  1. 30 mins

    Brief & land-based warm-up

    We set the goals for the session, followed by a land-based warm-up led by Masters Strength & Conditioning students from Southampton Solent University.

  2. 2 hours

    Pool session: drills, skills & filming

    Technique-focused drills and skills to improve efficiency. Around 30–45 minutes of this is filmed with underwater cameras and iPads above the water for on-site analysis of your technique.

  3. 20 mins

    Debrief

    We break down what you've learned, what the footage shows and the key points to take back to your own training.

  4. After

    Your videos & full report

    Once the session is complete, you'll receive your video footage and a full written report to keep working from.

Sneak peek

What your underwater footage looks like

From the dive entry to the touch, the underwater camera captures what you can't see from the poolside: your streamline, underwater kicks, breakout, catch and body position. These are the details we break down together in the debrief.
